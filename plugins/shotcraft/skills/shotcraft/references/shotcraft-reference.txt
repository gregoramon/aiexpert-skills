# SHOTCRAFT REFERENCE
Worked example: Single-Origin Espresso — 15s cinematic macro film

Use this as a calibration target for detail level, cinematic vocabulary, and structure.
The output for a real brief should match this density and specificity. The scenario is a
product/craft film, but the same approach scales to commercial, social vertical,
cinematic interview, and UGC — see the domain matrix in SKILL.md.

================================================================
SHOT-BY-SHOT TIMELINE
================================================================

SHOT 1 (00:00-00:01) — Drop on Hot Copper
• EFFECT: extreme macro + speed ramp (deceleration) + thermal haze
• A single droplet of water lands on the polished copper boiler of an espresso machine and flashes to vapor on contact
• Camera: 100mm macro, locked-off; frame composed tight on the contact point with the rest of the boiler falling into shallow defocus
• Light: single hard key from upper-left, deep falloff into a black surround; specular hit on the polished copper
• Grade: warm clean, lifted blacks, slight filmic halation around the highlight
• Speed: 240fps overcrank decelerating into the impact, then a brief beat at full slow-motion as the steam unfurls
• Exit: the steam plume rises and fills frame, becoming the wipe into Shot 2

SHOT 2 (00:01-00:03) — Tamp
• EFFECT: push-in dolly + shallow DOF
• A hand seats fresh grounds into a portafilter and tamps once, decisively; the tamper lifts with a quarter-turn polish
• Camera: 50mm at f/2, shoulder-height push-in toward the portafilter, parallax against the bar surface
• Light: tungsten practical from the espresso machine spilling warm onto the hand; softened key from camera-left
• Grade: warm clean, controlled highlights on the metal
• Speed: 24fps, real time
• Exit: tamper lifts away — quick cut on the upward motion

SHOT 3 (00:03-00:04) — Grinder Burst (SIGNATURE)
• EFFECT: 240fps deep slow-motion + radial motion blur + speed ramp on exit
• Coffee grounds explode outward from the grinder chute in a fine cloud, individual grains visible against a dark backdrop
• Camera: 85mm at f/2, locked-off, framed dead-centre with negative space top and bottom
• Light: single hard top-light, rim light from behind through the cloud
• Grade: high-contrast, near-bleach-bypass; grounds read as warm against black
• Speed: 240fps overcrank — the slowest moment in the film — ramping up to 24fps as the grounds settle
• Exit: speed ramp into Shot 4; the cloud snaps to stillness

SHOT 4 (00:04-00:05) — Lock-In
• EFFECT: top-down macro + locked-off
• Portafilter handle rotates into the espresso machine group head and locks home with a quarter-turn
• Camera: 100mm macro, top-down, dead vertical
• Light: tungsten machine practical underlighting the metal; cool fill from above
• Grade: warm clean
• Speed: 24fps, real time
• Exit: hard cut on the click

SHOT 5 (00:05-00:07) — First Drips
• EFFECT: anamorphic macro + 240fps deep slow-motion + rack focus
• Twin streams of espresso emerge from the portafilter spouts, dark amber tightening to mahogany; the first drops fall into a glass demitasse
• Camera: 100mm anamorphic-style (oval bokeh, horizontal flare on the rim light), eye-level with the spouts
• Light: hard rim from behind the glass, tungsten key from camera-left; glass rim catches a horizontal lens flare
• Grade: warm filmic, deep amber midtones
• Speed: 240fps overcrank, full deep slow-motion
• Exit: focus pulls from the spouts to the surface of the cup

SHOT 6 (00:07-00:08) — Crema Forming
• EFFECT: macro orbit + shallow DOF
• Crema swirls into a tiger-stripe pattern across the surface of the espresso
• Camera: 100mm macro on a slow ~30° orbit around the cup
• Light: single overhead soft key, no fill — strong falloff into a black surround
• Grade: warm clean, deep amber
• Speed: 48fps gentle overcrank
• Exit: motion-blur whip into Shot 7

SHOT 7 (00:08-00:10) — Barista, Profile
• EFFECT: 85mm shallow DOF + tungsten practical motivation
• Side profile of the barista, eyes down on the machine, steam rising past the face
• Camera: 85mm at f/1.4, locked-off, eye-level
• Light: tungsten practical from the machine motivating warm key on the cheek; cool ambient fill from the room
• Grade: split warm/cool — warm skin, cooler background
• Speed: 24fps, real time
• Exit: held beat, then soft cut

SHOT 8 (00:10-00:11) — Spoon Through Crema
• EFFECT: top-down macro + slow-motion
• A small spoon drags a slow line through the crema; the surface heals behind it
• Camera: 100mm macro, top-down, locked-off
• Light: single overhead soft key
• Grade: warm filmic
• Speed: 96fps overcrank
• Exit: hard cut

SHOT 9 (00:11-00:13) — Lift From Grate
• EFFECT: hard backlight + atmospheric steam + slow dolly-back
• The cup is lifted from the drip tray; thick steam catches the backlight and fills the frame
• Camera: 35mm, shoulder-height, slow dolly-back as the cup rises
• Light: single hard backlight from behind the machine, no fill — the steam reads as a luminous volume
• Grade: warm clean, blooming highlights
• Speed: 48fps gentle overcrank
• Exit: steam clears as the cup moves out of the machine area

SHOT 10 (00:13-00:14) — Placed on Saucer
• EFFECT: natural window light + locked-off
• The cup is set on a saucer in front of a seated customer; the saucer meets the wood with a small contact sound
• Camera: 50mm, locked-off, three-quarter angle
• Light: soft window light from camera-right, deep ambient shadow on the left
• Grade: warm clean, slightly desaturated
• Speed: 24fps, real time
• Exit: cup settles, beat held

SHOT 11 (00:14-00:15) — First Sip / Rack
• EFFECT: rack focus + held close
• The cup tilts to the lips; focus pulls from the rim of the cup to the eyes above it
• Camera: 85mm at f/1.4, slight tilt-up as the cup rises
• Light: same soft window light
• Grade: warm clean
• Speed: 24fps, real time
• Exit: hold on the eyes for the final half-second; fade to black
